Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to keeping data collection to a minimum. Depending on the specific App you are using, we may collect the following categories of information:

1.1 Information You Provide

Some of our Apps allow you to enter information such as notes, preferences, goals, or settings. This information is stored locally on your device by default and is not transmitted to us unless a specific feature (such as cloud backup or account sync) requires it and you have opted in.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Our Apps may automatically collect non-personal technical information, including:

  • Device type, model, and manufacturer
  • Operating system version and language
  • App version and installation ID
  • Anonymous crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • General usage statistics (such as features accessed and session duration)
  • Approximate (country/region) location derived from IP address

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address (unless you voluntarily provide them)
  • Your precise GPS location (unless a specific App feature explicitly requires it and you grant permission)
  • Your contacts, photos, or files (unless a specific feature requires access and you grant permission)
  • Sensitive personal information such as financial, health, biometric, or government identification data

2. How We Use Information

Any information collected is used only to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our Apps
  • Improve app stability, fix bugs, and enhance performance
  • Understand usage patterns in aggregate to improve features
  • Display relevant advertisements (in ad-supported Apps)
  • Process subscriptions and in-app purchases through official app stores
  • Respond to user support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Advertising

Some of our Apps are supported by advertising. We may use third-party advertising networks, including Google AdMob, which may use advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to serve ads that are relevant to you. These networks may collect information about your device and ad interactions in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by going to your device's Settings → Google → Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization," or resetting your advertising ID.

4. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Some of our Apps offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details. Subscription management (including cancellations and refunds) is handled through your Google Play account.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (if ads are shown)
  • Firebase / Google Analytics (for analytics and crash reporting)
  • Expo / EAS (if used for app development)
  • Google Play Billing (for subscriptions and purchases)

6. Permissions

Some Apps may request permissions (such as storage, camera, microphone, notifications, or internet access) to function properly. These permissions are used solely for the stated feature within the App and are never used for unrelated purposes. You can review and revoke any permission at any time through your device settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

Any data generated by our Apps is primarily stored locally on your device. When data is transmitted to our services or third parties (such as for cloud backup or analytics), we use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect it in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Some of our Apps may be directed to children under the age of 13. For these Apps, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not show behavioral or targeted advertising to children
  • We do not share data with third parties for advertising in children's Apps
  • Analytics in children's Apps are limited to supporting internal operations only
  • Apps directed at children do not include social features, external links, or in-app purchases without parental gates

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR (European Union), CCPA (California), PDPA, or other applicable privacy laws, including:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of data collection or personalized advertising
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

10. Data Retention

Since we collect minimal personal information, we retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Anonymous technical and analytics data is typically retained for up to 24 months. Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data.

11. International Data Transfers

Our Apps and third-party services may process data in countries other than your own. By using our Apps, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries, which may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable law.
